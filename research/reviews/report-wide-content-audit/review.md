## Architecture decision after audit

The strongest structure is eight chapters. The current organization chapter and competition-infrastructure chapter become one ecosystem chapter. The current dataset-content and dataset-access material remain separate but move to Chapters 6 and 7. Compatible performance change belongs only in Chapter 4; event-scale and institutional change belong only in Chapter 8. The Introduction becomes orientation rather than a miniature report.

1. Introduction — identity, scale, unusual breadth and the report’s question
2. Chapter 1 — event system: venue, participants, 30+21 structure, autonomy and platform model
3. Chapter 2 — mechanics and engineering capabilities across the 30 competition events
4. Chapter 3 — the 21 real-world tasks and concrete work sequences
5. Chapter 4 — results, compatible performance changes and team-level medals
6. Chapter 5 — teams, organizations, platforms and competition infrastructure
7. Chapter 6 — the 2,500+ hour dataset: production, contents and documented technical fields
8. Chapter 7 — practical access and reuse audit
9. Chapter 8 — event-scale change, post-event programs and synthesis
10. Appendix — full ledgers, method, audit detail and unresolved evidence

This decision is based on topic ownership, not the existing filenames. `docs/ch09.md` will be removed after its unique content is incorporated into Chapter 8.
