# Review: Kimi reader, dataset, and terminology imports

Review date: 2026-10-22

## Outcome

Accepted as immutable research snapshots and editorial evidence leads. No wholesale canonical promotion was performed. Verified facts are expressed in the report through underlying public sources; the workbooks themselves remain provenance-bearing inputs.

## Coverage review

- The event dossier contains 51 of 51 listed competitions, all 21 real-world task competitions, 42 candidate narratives, 34 evidence-map rows, and an explicit gap sheet.
- Six representative narrative clusters were checked for report use: 100-metre sprint, 1,500-metre endurance, 5-on-5 football, domestic service, industrial packing/storage, and dexterous-hand tasks.
- Competition and result claims were checked against official result pages, official summaries, institutional publications, or attributable reporting already represented in the event ledger. Workbook labels are not treated as official merely because they appear in a structured row.
- The 100-metre winning mark of 8.64 seconds, the 5-on-5 final score of 10–1, and the domestic-service winning result of 305 points in 26 minutes 12 seconds were retained after source review.
- Five competition-result source paths and 23 of 159 medal slots remain unresolved in the canonical reconstruction.

## Autonomy and remote operation

The workbook sometimes compresses operation mode into a narrative label. The report instead separates fully autonomous, remotely operated, mixed, and unestablished modes. The football case is supported as a fully autonomous match within its documented rules and result context. Domestic and industrial examples are scoped to the described task or run; they are not generalized across all 21 tasks.

## Dataset-publication audit

- The announced scale of more than 2,500 hours and the described modalities are retained as announcement/description claims.
- The seven named releasing organizations are listed as joint releasers. A specific data-contribution volume or role is not assigned without separate evidence.
- The access audit is dated 2026-10-13.
- A complete stable download path, retrievable package, manifest, version, checksums, schema documentation, and explicit reusable license were not jointly verified.
- RoboMIND, AgiBot World, Unitree releases, WorldSimReady-Home, and RekaDaily-10K are used only to compare publication mechanics.

## Terminology and identity

- Public prose uses Japanese canonical terms for competition families, operation modes, and real-world tasks.
- Official English names remain only where needed for dataset, product, or organization identification.
- Company brands, legal entities, teams, competition entries, and robot platforms remain distinct.
- The identity of Spirit AI with 万境千寻（北京）科技有限公司 is accepted for first-use identification based on the company's public registration disclosure. This does not establish a particular dataset contribution.
- Raw Simplified Chinese is confined to references, quotations, official-name annotations, and preserved identifiers.

## Corrections applied

- Replaced literal or awkward translations of the competition families with `競技部門` and `実環境タスク部門`.
- Replaced unqualified “open dataset” language with a four-state publication assessment.
- Removed implications that every named releaser necessarily contributed data in the same way.
- Kept official team, company, product, dataset, and source-title strings unchanged where identity depends on the original form.
- Routed full enumerations and audit mechanics to the appendix instead of the opening narrative.

## Promotion decision

Canonical records remain unchanged. The report-specific evidence ledger remains `research/evidence/event-report-2026.json`; the new imports are cited in this review by immutable hash. Future promotion requires a row-level proposal tied to an underlying public source, explicit entity mapping, duplicate analysis, and validation as a separate change.
